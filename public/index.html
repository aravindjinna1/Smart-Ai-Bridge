One Workspace for Every AI Model, Live Meetings, and Saved History

AI Bridge connects Gemini, DeepAI, ChatGPT, DeepSeek, Perplexity, Duck.ai, and Claude in a single interface. Listen to system speaker questions, read screen slides via local OCR, format answers for interviews, and keep your private chat history synced to MongoDB.

Core Capabilities

Built for real-time technical interviews, daily coding, and seamless multi-provider comparison.

01 · INTERVIEW MODE & FORMATTED CODE

Structured Interview Answers with Syntax Highlighting

Every technical question can be formatted into five clear sections — Definition, Purpose, Working, Practical MERN Example, and Important Point — in natural spoken English with colorized code blocks and one-click copy.

02 · SYSTEM SPEAKER AUDIO

Listen to Meet Speaker Output

Captures only computer speaker or tab audio from the other person on a call (never your microphone) and pastes the transcribed question straight into the input box.

03 · LOCAL SCREEN OCR

Read Screen & MCQ Solver

Extracts visible text, code snippets, and multiple-choice options locally in memory via Tesseract.js without sending raw screenshots to external servers.

04 · ISOLATED ACCOUNTS & HISTORY

Private MongoDB Atlas Chat History

Register with your account to unlock a ChatGPT-style collapsible sidebar. Each user’s conversations are stored separately, automatically titled, and accessible whenever you sign back in.
